# Pewhipee

**Your pictures, your memory.**

Pewhipee turns the photos already on your iPhone into memory games. No stock pictures. No strangers. No account.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6807252430)

## What it is

Each round starts with a photograph you took. The app scrambles it, hides someone, edits the light, asks who it is, or moves a subject — then you put the memory back.

Today’s challenge is one puzzle a day, built from your camera roll. Collect days in a memory jar. Missed days stay empty; they don’t wipe what you already have.

## Games

- **Scramble** — Cut a memory into tiles and rebuild it.
- **Match** — Pair each photo with a close-up cut out of it.
- **Who Is This?** — A photo reveals slowly. Guess who it is.
- **Spot the Difference** — The app really changed things. Find them.
- **Timeline** — Put memories in the order they happened.
- **Talking Memories** — Tell the story out loud, then prove you remember.
- **Hide** — A subject hides until you press.
- **Recreate** — Lift a subject and move it.
- **Darkroom** — We edit the picture. You put it back.
- **Typeset** — Words from your photo. Which typeface are they set in?
- **Gone** — Something was taken out. Find where it was.
- **Rewind** — A photo you edited, played backwards. What did you change?
- **Map Memory** — Drop a pin where you took this photo.

Play Easy, Medium, or Hard. Play alone, pass the phone, or run a gauntlet.

## Your photos stay here

Pewhipee has no server. Pictures are read on this iPhone, copied only if you keep them, and never written back to Photos. You choose which albums are fair game.

When you finish a round, you can send the puzzle as a picture. The other person opens it in Pewhipee. Nothing is uploaded along the way.

## Family

Pass and play is two people, one phone. Family trivia night uses everyone’s pictures on one device. There is no account to share and nothing to sync.
